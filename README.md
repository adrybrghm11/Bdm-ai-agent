# BDM AI Agent – Sales Workflow & Pipeline Optimization System

## Overview
This project is an AI-driven Business Development Manager (BDM) agent designed to streamline pipeline management, improve seller execution, and automate key aspects of the sales workflow.

The agent acts as a decision-support and automation layer, helping sales teams identify opportunities, maintain pipeline hygiene, and drive deal progression more effectively.

---

## Problem
Sales teams often face challenges such as:

- Inconsistent pipeline follow-up  
- Manual tracking of opportunities and next steps  
- Limited visibility into deal progression risks  
- Inefficient seller outreach and engagement workflows  

These inefficiencies can lead to stalled deals, missed opportunities, and reduced revenue impact.

---

## Solution
The BDM AI Agent introduces an intelligent workflow system that:

- Monitors pipeline activity and seller engagement  
- Identifies stalled or at-risk opportunities  
- Recommends next best actions for deal progression  
- Automates follow-ups and reminders  
- Standardizes pipeline execution across teams  

---

## How It Works

### Inputs
- Opportunity and pipeline data  
- Seller engagement activity  
- Product and SKU availability (where applicable)

### Processing
- Applies rule-based and logic-driven evaluation  
- Identifies pipeline gaps and inactivity  
- Maps opportunities to recommended actions  

### Outputs
- Seller notifications and reminders  
- Next-step recommendations (follow-up, requote, close strategy)  
- Alerts for at-risk or stalled opportunities

## Example Use Case

**Scenario:** Opportunity has had no seller activity for 10+ days  

**Agent Response:**
- Flags opportunity as at-risk  
- Sends seller notification  
- Recommends:
  - Customer follow-up  
  - Pricing / quote refresh  
  - Close plan development
